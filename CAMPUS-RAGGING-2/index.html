How The Reporting Process Works

A transparent 4-step framework designed to protect your identity while guaranteeing swift investigation and resolution.

01

1. File Confidential Report

Select the category, date, and location. Choose whether to submit anonymously or provide contact details for direct follow-up.

02

2. Receive Tracking Token

A unique, non-identifying tracking token (e.g. CRF-2026-8492) and scannable Token QR Code are generated instantly for you to keep safely.

03

3. Track Inquiry Status

Enter your token or scan your Token QR anytime to monitor inquiry milestones: Submitted, Under Review, Active Investigation, or Action Resolved.

04

4. Resolution & Support

The Anti-Ragging Committee takes disciplinary measures and provides physical, academic, and psychological assistance.

Our Solemn Commitment

Strict Privacy & Zero-Retaliation Policy

We understand that first-year students often fear social boycotts, intimidation, or victimization by senior students or hostel peers. Our portal was engineered from the ground up to eliminate that fear.

🛡️

Anonymous Safeguards

When you choose anonymous mode, no identifying student details, IP addresses, or browser footprints are stored or shared with anyone.

⚖️

Statutory Legal Protection

In accordance with Supreme Court mandates & UGC Anti-Ragging Regulations 2009, retaliatory acts against complainants are non-bailable offenses.

🔐

Token-Based Separation

Inquiries are assigned random alphanumeric tokens. The inquiry committee evaluates the facts without biased student profiling.

🤝

Direct Proctorial Care

Should you request identity disclosure at a later stage, your statements are recorded in camera in a confidential proctor chamber.

UGC Compliance Notice

What Constitutes Ragging?

  • ✕ Forced physical labor, cleaning senior rooms, or running unauthorized errands.
  • ✕ Verbal insults, derogatory slang, or humiliation based on origin, language, or appearance.
  • ✕ Hostel night raids, forcing students to sing/dance, or depriving them of sleep.
  • ✕ Academic extortion such as completing assignments or test manipulation for others.
Report without hesitation. Campus authorities stand firmly with you.
